# ServerMagic Wiki

Welcome to **ServerMagic**! This mod introduces a deep, progression-based magic system that integrates directly with Minecraft's advancement and experience systems.

## 🌟 Getting Started: Unlocking Magic

Magic is not available immediately. To begin your journey into the arcane, you must **reach the End**.

*   **Initial Unlock**: Completing the "The End" advancement (entering the End dimension) unlocks the base magic trees.
*   **The Four Disciplines**: Once unlocked, you gain access to four primary magic trees:
    *   **Elemental**: Offensive spells like Firebolts, Lightning Strikes, and Ice.
    *   **Utility**: Conjure bound tools (pickaxes, swords) or summon a mount.
    *   **Effects**: Self and group buffs like Speed, Invisibility, and Regeneration.
    *   **Building**: Spells that help you construct structures (lines, rings, squares).

## 📖 The Spellbook & Casting

All magic is channeled through a **Spellbook**. Without it, you cannot cast spells.

### Crafting the Spellbook
The Spellbook is simple to craft:
*   **Recipe**: Place a **Book** in any crafting grid (Shapeless).

### How to Cast
Casting in ServerMagic uses a unique **Combo System**. While holding your Spellbook, you perform a sequence of clicks to trigger a spell.

1.  **Initiate**: Every spell starts with a **Right-Click** (or **Shift + Right-Click**).
2.  **Sequence**: Follow up with two more clicks (Left or Right).
3.  **Completion**: Once the 3-click combo is finished, the spell is cast!

**Example Combos**:
*   `Right -> Right -> Right` (RRR)
*   `Right -> Left -> Right` (RLR)
*   `Shift + Right -> Right -> Right` (SRRR)

Your current combo progress is displayed on your **Action Bar** (above your health/hunger).

## ⚡ Mana & Experience

In ServerMagic, your **Experience (XP) is your Mana**.

*   **Casting Cost**: Every spell consumes a portion of your XP. Some spells have a flat cost, while others consume a percentage of your current level's requirement.
*   **Mana Potions**: You can craft **Mana Potions** (Bottles o' Enchanting) to replenish your "**mana**".
    *   **Recipe A**: Amethyst Shard + Lapis Lazuli + 2 Glass Bottles = 2 Mana Potions.
    *   **Recipe B**: Gold Ingot + Glowstone Dust + 2 Glass Bottles = 2 Mana Potions.
*   **💡 Pro Tip**: Mana Potions provide a fixed amount of XP. Because high-level spells cost more absolute XP, these potions are **most effective when you are below Level 10**.

## 🔍 Progression & Hidden Secrets

While many skills are unlocked via standard Minecraft advancements (e.g., getting a Blaze Rod unlocks Firebolt), some require **other specific actions**. The skill tree will tell you just what you need to do to unlock the skill. Or the very least a hint...

There are also some *secret* skills you can unlock. These do not appear in the regular skill tree, but will show up under the **Secrets** tab. You'll just have to discover these yourself by playing the game.

Check the **Skills** tab in the Web Portal to start unlocking magic!
