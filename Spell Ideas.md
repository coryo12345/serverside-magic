Projectile & Direct Damage

    Void Rift: Fire a slow-moving Wither Skull. When it hits, it creates a small explosion and applies 3 seconds of Blindness and Wither II.
    This can replace the existing WitherBlast spell.

    Frost Nova: A short-range blast that pushes all nearby enemies back and applies Slowness V for 2 seconds.

Area of Effect (AoE) & Ground Effects

    Earthen Spike: Summon a line of Evoker Fangs in the direction you are facing.

    Gravity Well: Use a particle circle on the ground. Every tick, use teleport or velocity to pull all nearby mobs toward the center.

    Ring of Fire: Create a circle of Fire blocks (or campfire particles) around the caster. Any mob entering the circle is set ablaze.

    Meteor Shower: Randomly spawn 5–10 Fireball entities high above a target location, falling straight down with high velocity.

    Desecrated Ground: Apply the Wither effect to any mob standing within a specific 5×5 area defined by black particles.

Crowd Control & Utility Combat

    Spectral Grasp: Teleport a target mob 5 blocks into the air and freeze them there for 2 seconds before letting them fall.

    Leech Link: A beam (Guardian laser or particles) that connects you to a mob; it takes damage every second, and you regain half a heart.

    Iron Maiden: Surround a mob with 8 Iron Bars. After 2 seconds, the bars vanish and the mob takes a large burst of "piercing" damage.

    Wind Gust: A powerful knockback blast in a cone. It deals no damage but flings mobs 10+ blocks away—great for cliffs.

Creative & Strategic Spells

    Shadow Step: Teleport behind the mob you are looking at and give your next hit a Strength II buff for 1 second.

    Bee Swarm: Summon 5 temporary Bees with a "target" tag that immediately aggro onto the nearest enemy.

    Sunbeam: A vertical pillar of "End Gateway" or "Flame" particles. Any undead mob inside takes massive Smite-style damage.

    Reap: A sweeping-edge particle effect in a wide arc. Any mob under 20% health is instantly killed; otherwise, it deals minor damage.
